# Preset changelog

Every change to a preset file needs an entry here and an updated `presets.lock.json` (`bun run presets:lock`).
Plans keep a snapshot of the preset they were created from, so students only see changes once plan updates exist.

## 2026-09-13 (attempt limits and PO switch)

- `luh/technische-informatik-bsc-2026`: attempt limits per § 14 Abs. 1 (3 attempts, Bachelorarbeit 2, no retakes of passed exams) and the Ergänzungsprüfung in the last Klausur attempt (§ 14 Abs. 4).
- `example/informatik-bsc-example-2027`: fictional successor PO with a transition from `example/informatik-bsc-example`, for tests and demos of the PO switch.

## 2026-09-13

- `luh/technische-informatik-bsc-2026`: added. Sources: PO Technische Informatik B.Sc. in force from 01.10.2026 (Verkündungsblatt 12/2026), Modulkatalog WS 2026/27 (Stand 07.09.2026), Studienverlaufsplan v8.7.2026. Includes the 7-day Klausur withdrawal rule (§ 15 Abs. 2).
- `example/informatik-bsc-example`: fictional preset for tests and demos.
